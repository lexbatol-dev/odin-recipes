Spaghetti Carbonara

Description

This dish is a creamy bowl of spaghetti carbonara made with a rich egg and cheese sauce. It is mixed with slices of sausage and topped with fresh parsley for extra flavor and color. It is served with a refreshing glass of lemon water, making it a simple and delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • 400g spaghetti
  • 150g sausage, sliced into rounds
  • 4 large eggs
  • 100g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • Salt for pasta water
  • Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish

Steps

  1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until soft. Save 1 cup of the pasta water before draining.
  2. While the pasta cooks, cook the sausage slices in a pan until browned. Add the garlic for the last minute.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the eggs and grated cheese together.
  4. Turn off the heat, then mix the hot pasta with the sausage. Quickly stir in the egg mixture, adding a little pasta water at a time until creamy.
  5. Add black pepper, top with fresh parsley, and serve right away.
