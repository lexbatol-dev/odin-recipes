Jollof Rice Recipe

Description

This is a plate of jollof rice, a smoky and flavorful West African rice dish cooked in a tomato-based sauce. It is served with a grilled chicken leg and a side of stir-fried peppers and mushrooms, making it a filling and tasty meal.

Ingredients

For the jollof rice

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 4 large tomatoes, blended
  • 2 red bell peppers, blended
  • 1 onion, chopped (plus extra for blending)
  • 2-3 scotch bonnet peppers (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken stock
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp thyme
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • Salt and seasoning cubes, to taste

For the grilled chicken

  • 4 chicken legs
  • 1 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil

For the stir-fried peppers and mushrooms

  • 1 cup mushrooms, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Steps

  1. Blend the tomatoes, red peppers, scotch bonnet, and half the onion into a smooth mix. Set aside.
  2. Heat oil in a pot, fry the chopped onion until soft, then stir in tomato paste and fry for a few minutes.
  3. Pour in the blended pepper mix and cook down until thickened, about 15-20 minutes.
  4. Add stock, bay leaves, thyme, curry powder, salt, and seasoning cubes. Bring to a boil.
  5. Stir in the rice, cover, and cook on low heat until tender, stirring occasionally.
  6. Meanwhile, season the chicken legs with paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Grill or pan-fry until fully cooked and browned on both sides.
  7. In a separate pan, stir-fry the mushrooms and green pepper in oil with a pinch of salt and pepper until softened.
  8. Serve the jollof rice with the grilled chicken and stir-fried vegetables on the side.
