Lasagna Recipe

Description

This is a golden, cheesy baked lasagna with crispy , bubbbly layer of melted cheese on top. It is baked until the edges are slightly browned, giving it a rich and appetizing look.

Fresh basil is placed in the center for a pop of color and flavor. It is served with soft slices of bread and a fresh cucumber, onion, and tomato salad, making it a warm and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 chopped onion and 3 minced garlic cloves
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes and 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 450g ricotta cheese and 1 large egg
  • 3 cups shredded mozzarella and 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
  • 9-12 lasagna noodles
  • Fresh basil leaves for garnish

Steps

  1. Cook the ground beef with onions and garlic, then simmer with the tomatoes and paste for 15 minutes.
  2. Mix the ricotta, egg, and parmesan together in a separate bowl.
  3. Preheat oven to 190°C. Layer sauce, noodles, ricotta mix, and mozzarella in a baking dish. Repeat layers.
  4. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake for 10 more minutes until golden.
  5. Let rest for 10 minutes, top with fresh basil leaves, and serve.
